This document is fictional test data for software development. It is not a legal form or legal advice.

1. PARTIES AND PREMISES

1.1 Landlord. North Star Property LLC ("Landlord").

1.2 Tenant. Jordan Reed ("Tenant").

1.3 Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant Apartment 2B at 101 Example Street, Sample City, Ohio 44000, together with the right to use the common hallway and common rear yard (the "Premises").

2. TERM

2.1 The lease begins September 1, 2026 and ends August 31, 2027 unless ended earlier according to this agreement or applicable law.

3. RENT, FEES, AND PAYMENT

3.1 Tenant shall pay Landlord rent of $900.00 per month.

3.2 Rent is due on the first calendar day of each month.

3.3 Tenant shall pay rent through the payment method designated in writing by Landlord and shall retain a receipt or other payment confirmation.

3.4 Tenant shall pay for electricity and internet service. Landlord shall pay for water and sewer service.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $900.00 before receiving possession of the Premises.

4.2 After the tenancy ends, Landlord shall provide any deposit accounting and return required by the governing law. This clause intentionally does not state a numeric deadline.

5. MOVE-IN BASELINE

5.1 Within three calendar days after receiving possession, Tenant shall complete the move-in condition checklist and may attach photographs of existing conditions.

5.2 Landlord shall preserve the completed checklist with the lease records.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

6.1 Landlord shall maintain the roof, exterior walls, plumbing, heating, and electrical systems in safe working order, subject to applicable law and damage caused by Tenant.

6.2 Landlord shall maintain the common rear yard, including removal or trimming of vegetation that grows against the building.

6.3 Tenant shall keep the apartment reasonably clean, dispose of waste properly, and avoid damaging the Premises.

6.4 Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing after discovering a leak, pest entry point, structural opening, loss of heat, electrical hazard, or other condition that may require repair.

6.5 Tenant is responsible for damage caused by Tenant's intentional act, negligence, or misuse, excluding ordinary wear.

7. SAFETY CHECKS

7.1 On the first Saturday of each month, Tenant shall test the smoke detector using its test button.

7.2 If the smoke detector does not sound, Tenant shall notify Landlord in writing on the same day.

7.3 Landlord shall repair or replace a reported nonworking smoke detector promptly.

8. ENTRY AND INSPECTION

8.1 Except in an emergency, Landlord shall give Tenant at least 24 hours' written notice before entering the apartment for inspection, repair, or showing.

8.2 Tenant shall permit lawful entry at a reasonable time after required notice is given.

9. COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

9.1 Routine notices and repair requests shall be sent by email.

9.2 Landlord notice email: manager@example.test.

9.3 Tenant notice email: jordan.reed@example.test.

9.4 Emergency conditions involving active fire, flooding, gas odor, or immediate danger shall be reported by telephone first and confirmed by email as soon as reasonably possible.

9.5 A sender shall retain a copy of each significant notice and any available transmission confirmation.

10. INSURANCE

10.1 Tenant shall maintain renters insurance during the lease term and shall retain evidence of current coverage.

11. RENEWAL AND TERMINATION PLANNING

11.1 Either party intending not to renew shall give written notice at least 30 calendar days before the lease end date, unless governing law requires a different period.

11.2 No notice generated by software replaces a delivery method required by this agreement or governing law.

12. MOVE-OUT

12.1 Before returning possession, Tenant shall remove personal property and leave the apartment reasonably clean, excluding ordinary wear.

12.2 The parties may conduct a move-out inspection and compare the condition with the move-in checklist and photographs.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND AMENDMENTS

13.1 This document and any signed addenda are the agreement between the parties concerning the Premises.

13.2 An amendment must be in writing and approved by both parties. A later amendment does not erase the prior version from the parties' records.
