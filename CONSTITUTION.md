# Tekmerion Constitution

## Preamble

Agreements are meant to create peace and predictability. They fail when their terms disappear from ordinary life, performance is forgotten, communication becomes fragmented, or memory replaces evidence.

Tekmerion exists to keep agreements present. It helps a person understand what has been agreed, remember what is due, document faithful performance, report problems, and preserve a trustworthy contemporaneous record.

The product is designed first for stewardship, not accusation; for clarity, not escalation; and for dignity, not control.

## Article I — Agreement First

1. Every Tekmerion record begins with an agreement, obligation, or other source of responsibility.
2. The agreement remains a living reference throughout the relationship rather than a document consulted only during conflict.
3. Every derived obligation must retain provenance to the exact source document, version, and clause whenever available.
4. Tekmerion must distinguish contractual obligations from statutory duties, user-entered expectations, and machine-generated candidates.

## Article II — Faithful Performance Is Visible

1. Tekmerion must make it easy to document how a user keeps an agreement.
2. Evidence of performance is not secondary to evidence of failure.
3. The product should help users demonstrate payment, reporting, maintenance, notice, cooperation, and other fulfilled duties.
4. The resulting record should benefit every party by reducing uncertainty and reconstructive disputes.

## Article III — Contemporaneous by Default

1. Users should be encouraged to document events as close as reasonably possible to when they occur.
2. `occurred_at` and `recorded_at` must remain distinct.
3. The interface must not imply that a later recollection was made contemporaneously.
4. Timezone and relevant source metadata should be preserved.

## Article IV — Originals Are Preserved

1. Imported agreements, photos, videos, receipts, messages, and documents must remain available in their original form.
2. Finalized records must not be silently overwritten.
3. Corrections must create linked revisions while preserving the original record.
4. Evidence hashing may demonstrate file identity inside Tekmerion, but must not be presented as notarization, certified timestamping, or guaranteed admissibility.

## Article V — Facts, Assertions, and Interpretations Remain Distinct

1. Tekmerion must distinguish firsthand observations, source documents, transactions, communications, another party’s assertions, user interpretations, legal authorities, and outcomes.
2. The product must not flatten those categories into a single authoritative narrative.
3. Machine-generated extraction must be labeled as a candidate until confirmed by the user.
4. Uncertainty and ambiguity must remain visible.

## Article VI — Human Confirmation Governs Meaning

1. OCR, segmentation, classification, and extraction may be probabilistic.
2. Probabilistic output must never silently become a confirmed obligation.
3. The user must confirm responsibility, due dates, recurrence, and meaning when ambiguity exists.
4. Once confirmed, operational reminders and status changes should be deterministic and reproducible.

## Article VII — Peace Before Litigation

1. Tekmerion’s first purpose is to reduce agreement-related anxiety and prevent avoidable conflict.
2. The product may support factual notice, response, negotiation, mediation, and formal-process records, but should not reward escalation.
3. Tekmerion must not generate automatic accusations, public shaming, harassment, or retaliation tooling.
4. Litigation readiness is a consequence of good recordkeeping, not the product’s primary identity.

## Article VIII — Neutrality and Dignity

1. Tekmerion may document any party’s fulfillment, non-fulfillment, response, or disagreement.
2. The product may recognize power asymmetry without dehumanizing either party.
3. Status labels must remain factual and restrained.
4. Tekmerion must not declare a person guilty, negligent, unlawful, or in breach unless a qualified external authority has made that determination and the user records it as such.

## Article IX — Privacy and Consent

1. Tekmerion v1 is private by default and local-first.
2. No record becomes visible to another tenant, landlord, organization, or community without an explicit user action.
3. Future sharing must be granular: sharing one item must not expose the full agreement or evidence library.
4. Consent grants and revocations should themselves be recorded.
5. Future shared records must preserve who observed what, when, and with what evidence.

## Article X — User Ownership and Portability

1. Users own and control their records.
2. Tekmerion must support full export in ordinary, portable formats.
3. The product must not hold a person’s agreement history hostage to a proprietary format or subscription.
4. Deletion must be explicit, informed, and preceded by an opportunity to export.
5. No automatic evidence deletion may occur without clear user consent.

## Article XI — Jurisdictional Humility

1. Tekmerion organizes agreements and records; it does not provide legal advice.
2. Deadlines, remedies, notice procedures, retention periods, and legal duties vary by jurisdiction.
3. General templates and suggestions must be labeled accordingly.
4. Jurisdiction-specific guidance may ship only after validation against authoritative primary or official sources.
5. Tekmerion must not guarantee legal sufficiency, admissibility, service, or outcome.

## Article XII — Product Freeze

Tekmerion v1 is governed by the following loop:

```text
upload → confirm obligation → remind → document → link to clause → export
```

New ideas are deferred unless they are required to complete that loop reliably.

### Included in v1

- agreement import;
- text extraction and clause segmentation;
- reviewed obligation candidates;
- deterministic reminders;
- photo, receipt, document, and note capture;
- clause-linked finalized records;
- timeline and export;
- local backup.

### Deferred beyond v1

- cloud accounts and synchronization;
- automatic email sending;
- landlord portals;
- shared tenant communities;
- public ratings or scoring;
- conversational legal advice;
- autonomous contract interpretation;
- legal outcome prediction.

## Article XIII — Sustainable Access

1. The free spreadsheet teaches and supports the manual workflow.
2. The paid app earns its price through automation, reminders, capture, provenance, integrity, and export.
3. The app should save time and reduce burden rather than restrict access to a person’s own records.

## Closing Principle

> Tekmerion keeps agreements present in ordinary life so that faithful performance is visible, ambiguity is reduced, and accountability does not begin only after trust has failed.
